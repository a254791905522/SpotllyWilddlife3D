How to play

  1. Enter a habitat — Explore a 3D scene filled with life-like motion and natural detail.
  2. Scan the biome — Pan and study the environment to catch animals that use camouflage and cover.
  3. Spot and identify — Tap or mark rare species before they slip out of view.
  4. Use boosters wisely — Reveal hidden wildlife, improve visibility, or gain time when a level gets tough.
