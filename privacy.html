Section 1

What this policy covers

Spotlly Wildlife 3D is a mobile game distributed on the Apple App Store. This overview describes privacy practices for that product only. It does not apply to unrelated websites or services.

If anything here differs from the installed app or Apple’s “App Privacy” section on the store listing, treat the store label and on-device behavior as the source of truth.

Section 2

Data collection summary

According to the App Store privacy label for Spotlly Wildlife 3D, the developer states that no data is collected from the app for tracking or linking purposes as defined in that label.

Apple may still process download, purchase, and account information under its own policies when you use the App Store. That processing is outside the game’s direct control.

Section 3

Information on your device

Gameplay progress, settings, and similar session data are typically stored locally in the app sandbox on your iPhone or iPad so you can continue your wildlife expeditions without creating an in-game account.

Removing the app from your device, or clearing its data in iOS Settings, deletes that local information from your phone or tablet.

Section 4

Advertising

The App Store listing indicates that the game may contain advertising. When an ad is shown, the ad network involved may process data under its own privacy rules and Apple’s advertising frameworks.

You can review and adjust ad-related options in iOS Settings where your system version and region make them available (for example, privacy controls and content restrictions).

Section 5

Children and families

The game is rated 4+ on the App Store. Parents and guardians should supervise younger players and use Screen Time or similar tools if they want to limit play time or purchases.

We do not knowingly design Spotlly Wildlife 3D to require children to submit personal contact details inside the app for basic play.

Section 6

Your choices

  • Delete the app to remove locally stored game data from your device.
  • Check iOS Settings → Privacy & Security for permissions and tracking options that apply to apps on your device.
  • Read the current App Store “App Privacy” panel after each major update in case practices change.
Section 7

Questions and updates

For product support or privacy questions about Spotlly Wildlife 3D, use the support channel provided on the App Store listing for this title.

We may update this page when features or disclosures change. Material changes should also be reflected on the App Store when Apple requires updated privacy information.

This page is a plain-language summary for Spotlly Wildlife 3D. It intentionally omits personal developer contact details and third-party homepages. It is not a substitute for professional legal advice.
